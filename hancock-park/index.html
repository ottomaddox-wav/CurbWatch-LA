Street parking in Hancock Park, block by block: when each street is swept, which streets are permit-only, where the meters are, and which blocks draw the most tickets.

Meters

248 metered spaces; hourly rates run from $1.00 to $1.50; time limits 2 hours, 1 hour. Meters run Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM on almost every block (going by when meter tickets are written); outside those hours parking at a meter is free unless a sign says otherwise. Rates vary by time of day. Live space-by-space availability is on the map.

What this page can’t tell you

Of 327 blocks, 3 have tickets but no rule we can point to (a sign probably exists that the city’s map doesn’t carry), and 2 have no records at all. No record is not the same as free parking. No dataset covers red curbs, hydrants, driveways, loading zones, or temporary no-parking signs. Terms & data notes explain what is inferred and what is official.
