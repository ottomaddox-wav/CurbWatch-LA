Parking in Downtown LA

Updated Oct 26, 2026 · ticket records through Sep 9, 2026

Street parking in Downtown LA, block by block: when each street is swept, which streets are permit-only, where the meters are, and which blocks draw the most tickets.

The posted sign is the authority. This page is not. It is built from public records and can be out of date or wrong. Read the curb before you leave your car.

See Downtown LA on the map

The map stops around 7th–8th Street, so the Fashion District, South Park and most of the Arts District aren’t covered.

Downtown is also swept overnight, 1–4 AM on weekdays, on routes the city doesn’t class as posted no-parking routes, so they aren’t listed here. If your block has a sign, the sign applies.

At a glance

  • 547 street blocks mapped in Downtown LA; 251 (46%) have a mapped rule: sweeping, meters, permit enforcement or a game-day zone.
  • Street sweeping is posted on 21 streets. By block, the days are Friday 97%, Wednesday 44%, Thursday 24%.
  • 2,133 parking meters.
  • 2 streets where permit-parking tickets are written routinely.
  • 56,535 parking tickets written here in the six months to Sep 9, 2026.

Meters

2,133 metered spaces; hourly rates run from $0.50 to $8.00; time limits 2 hours, 4 hours, 10 hours, 6 hours. Meters run Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM on almost every block (going by when meter tickets are written); outside those hours parking at a meter is free unless a sign says otherwise. Rates vary by time of day. Live space-by-space availability is on the map.

What this page can’t tell you

Of 547 blocks, 213 have tickets but no rule we can point to (a sign probably exists that the city’s map doesn’t carry), and 83 have no records at all. No record is not the same as free parking. No dataset covers red curbs, hydrants, driveways, loading zones, or temporary no-parking signs. Terms & data notes explain what is inferred and what is official.
