Speed cameras in Los Angeles

Updated Oct 26, 2026

LADOT’s speed-camera program switched on its first corridors in late 2026. 12 of them run through CurbWatch’s map area. Here’s where they are and when fines start.

See them on the map

How the tickets work

  • Cameras ticket drivers going 11 mph or more over the limit, by mail to the registered owner.
  • Fines: $50 up to 15 mph over, $100 up to 25 over, $200 above that, and $500 at 100 mph or more.
  • Each corridor starts with 60 days of warning notices before fines begin.
