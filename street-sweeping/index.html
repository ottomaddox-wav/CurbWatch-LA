Street sweeping in Los Angeles

Updated Oct 26, 2026 · ticket records through Sep 9, 2026

Los Angeles posts a street-sweeping window on most residential streets: one day, a few hours, no parking. Miss it and the ticket is $73. Here’s how the schedule works from Echo Park to West Hollywood, with every street’s day and hours on its neighborhood page.

The posted sign is the authority. This page is not. It is built from public records and can be out of date or wrong. Read the curb before you leave your car.

Check your block on the map

How LA street sweeping works

  • The sign is the schedule. Posted sweeping signs give a day and a time window. The city’s route data is what this site uses, and it matches the signs on most blocks, but not all.
  • One side per day. Many streets are swept on two days, one side each day. The city’s data doesn’t say which side, so read the sign on your side.
  • Twice a month, not every week. Nearly all swept blocks are on a 1st & 3rd week or 2nd & 4th week schedule, so check which weeks your sign says, not just the day.
  • Windows run 2 or 3 hours. The most common are noon–3 PM, 8 AM–10 AM, 10 AM–noon, 8 AM–11 AM.
  • City holidays suspend sweeping. Other days, the posted window applies whether or not you see the sweeper.
  • West Hollywood is a separate city with its own schedule: see parking in West Hollywood.

Which days

Across the map, 7,974 Los Angeles blocks have posted sweeping. By block, the days are Monday 46%, Tuesday 46%, Thursday 35%, Wednesday 33%, Friday 33%.

72,429 street-cleaning tickets were written on these blocks between Mar 12, 2026 and Sep 9, 2026.
