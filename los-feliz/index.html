Meters

328 metered spaces; hourly rates run from $1.50 to $1.50; time limits 2 hours, 1 hour, 15 min. Meters run Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM on almost every block (going by when meter tickets are written); outside those hours parking at a meter is free unless a sign says otherwise. Rates vary by time of day. Live space-by-space availability is on the map.

What this page can’t tell you

Of 603 blocks, 34 have tickets but no rule we can point to (a sign probably exists that the city’s map doesn’t carry), and 164 have no records at all. No record is not the same as free parking. No dataset covers red curbs, hydrants, driveways, loading zones, or temporary no-parking signs. Terms & data notes explain what is inferred and what is official.
