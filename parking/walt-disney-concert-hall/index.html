Meters

119 metered spaces; hourly rates run from $0.50 to $7.00; time limits 2 hours, 4 hours, 30 min, 15 min. Meters run Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM on almost every block (going by when meter tickets are written); outside those hours parking at a meter is free unless a sign says otherwise.
