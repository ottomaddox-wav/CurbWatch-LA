Street parking in Chinatown, block by block: when each street is swept, which streets are permit-only, where the meters are, and which blocks draw the most tickets.

The posted sign is the authority. This page is not. It is built from public records and can be out of date or wrong. Read the curb before you leave your car.

See Chinatown on the map

About 89% of Chinatown, by the LA Times outline, falls inside the map’s coverage; streets outside it aren’t listed.

At a glance

  • 189 street blocks mapped in Chinatown; 147 (78%) have a mapped rule: sweeping, meters, permit enforcement or a game-day zone.
  • Street sweeping is posted on 45 streets. By block, the days are Thursday 92%, Friday 81%, Wednesday 12%, Tuesday 11%.
  • 534 parking meters.
  • 5,140 parking tickets written here in the six months to Sep 9, 2026.

Meters

534 metered spaces; hourly rates run from $0.50 to $5.00; time limits 2 hours, 4 hours, 10 hours, 30 min. Meters run Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–8 PM on almost every block (going by when meter tickets are written); outside those hours parking at a meter is free unless a sign says otherwise. Rates vary by time of day. Live space-by-space availability is on the map.

What this page can’t tell you

Of 189 blocks, 24 have tickets but no rule we can point to (a sign probably exists that the city’s map doesn’t carry), and 18 have no records at all. No record is not the same as free parking.
