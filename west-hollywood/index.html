Parking in West Hollywood

Updated Oct 26, 2026

West Hollywood is its own city, with its own permit districts and its own sweeping schedule. This page uses the City of West Hollywood’s published data, which, unlike Los Angeles, lists the posted permit hours block by block.

The posted sign is the authority. This page is not. It is built from public records and can be out of date or wrong. Read the curb before you leave your car.

See West Hollywood on the map

At a glance

  • 419 permit-parking blocks across 13 districts (1R, 2R, 3R, 4R, 5R, 6R, 6R/7R, 7R, 7R/8R, 8R, 9R, 10R, 11R).
  • Some districts are permit-only around the clock; on most blocks, non-residents get a 2-hour limit by day and permit-only parking at night.
  • Street sweeping is posted on 718 street segments, on 23 schedules.
  • 15 city-run parking structures and lots.
  • West Hollywood’s parking tickets aren’t in Los Angeles’ ticket data, so this page has no ticket history.
